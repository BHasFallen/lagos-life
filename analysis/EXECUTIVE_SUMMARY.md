# Lagos Life — Executive Summary & Product Teardown

## 1. Overview
**Lagos Life** (`https://lagoslife.eliysites.com` / `https://lagoslife.app`) is an isometric 3D multiplayer life simulation game set in Lagos, Nigeria. Designed as a culturally authentic "Naija Sims", the game allows players to live out realistic Nigerian metropolitan experiences directly within the web browser without installing an app.

Players navigate the vibrant hustle of Lagos: riding yellow *danfo* minibuses, attending lectures at the University of Lagos (UNILAG), eating steaming *amala & ewedu* at Shitta in Surulere, parting at Quilox on Victoria Island, spraying money at parties, surviving NEPA electricity blackouts with generators, and even voting in player-driven gubernatorial elections.

## 4. What Makes Lagos Life Unique?
1. **Unrivaled Cultural Authenticity**:
   Rather than cloning generic Western life sims, every interaction, sound, food item, location, and economic stressor is rooted in contemporary Nigerian urban reality:
   - **NEPA Power Outages**: Unpowered homes lose fridge food and electronics unless the player buys a "Tiger generator" (*I pass my neighbor*), diesel lister, or solar inverter.
   - **Owambe & Money Spraying**: Players can throw cash into the air at nightclubs and parties; onlookers can physically pick up the sprayed notes from the floor.
   - **Academic Hustle at UNILAG**: Attending 100L classes, studying TDB (*Till Day Break*), dealing with ASUU strikes, and graduating with a First Class degree that permanently boosts job salaries by 20%.
   - **Lapo Microloans & Nepo Cash**: Players can start from humble origins (Ajegunle hustler with ₦5k) and take emergency LAPO loans, or play as an Ikoyi Nepo Baby with ₦500k and a Toyota Prado.

2. **Native Web-Based 3D Engine**:
   Runs 100% in mobile and desktop browsers via Three.js / React Three Fiber without requiring app store installation or downloads. Utilizes lightweight modular low-poly 3D models with palette texture atlasing for fast loading over Nigerian mobile data connections (MTN, Airtel, Glo).

3. **In-Game Commercial Ad Network**:
   The 3D game world is lined with 38 physical 3D highway billboards and 595 floating sea billboards in the Lagos Lagoon. Real Nigerian startups (Salary Index, Chowdeck, KudiSMS, FastDrive, ThriftyHustle), Afrobeats musicians, fashion designers, and local food vendors pay to advertise their links and social media inside the game.
