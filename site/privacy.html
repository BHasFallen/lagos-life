This Privacy & Cookie Policy explains how Lagos Life collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when you use Lagos Life through lagoslife.app, lagoslife.eliysites.com, and related services.

Lagos Life is operated by Shalom Rayhamen, Nigeria.

For purposes of applicable data protection law, Shalom Rayhamen determines the purposes and means by which personal data is processed in connection with Lagos Life, except where another service provider acts independently under its own legal obligations.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to information processed in connection with:

  • your Lagos Life account;
  • gameplay;
  • social features;
  • payments;
  • support;
  • abuse prevention;
  • advertising services;
  • partnerships;
  • websites and web applications;
  • cookies and similar technologies; and
  • other Lagos Life services.

Lagos Life is intended for persons aged 18 and over.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Lagos Life, we may collect the following categories of information.

Account Information

This may include:

  • display name;
  • username or handle;
  • email address;
  • password or password authentication data;
  • account identifiers;
  • account creation date;
  • account status; and
  • authentication and security information.

Passwords should be stored using appropriate cryptographic protection rather than as readable plaintext.

Avatar Information

During avatar customization, you may select:

  • man; or
  • woman.

This selection is used for avatar customization and related gameplay purposes.

Gameplay Information

We may process information concerning your activity inside Lagos Life, including:

  • Game Naira balances;
  • virtual transactions;
  • player-to-player transfers;
  • purchases;
  • jobs;
  • activities;
  • skills;
  • experience;
  • virtual assets;
  • properties;
  • investments;
  • interactions;
  • relationships;
  • venue activity;
  • game progress;
  • achievements;
  • economic activity;
  • penalties;
  • game events; and
  • other actions within the game world.

Social and User Content

Where you use Lagos Life social features, we may process:

  • public messages;
  • venue chat;
  • direct messages;
  • posts;
  • comments;
  • reactions;
  • stickers;
  • usernames;
  • profiles;
  • uploaded content where supported;
  • reports;
  • blocks;
  • moderation records; and
  • other communications made through the game.

Other players may see information that you intentionally make public or provide through public game features.

Payment Information

Payments are processed through Flutterwave (and, for payments made before 6 October 2026, Bachs).

Lagos Life may receive information such as:

  • transaction reference;
  • amount;
  • currency;
  • payment status;
  • payment time;
  • product purchased;
  • customer or transaction identifier; and
  • information required to reconcile or investigate a transaction.

Payment card, bank, or other payment credentials may be processed directly by Flutterwave (or Bachs) or their payment infrastructure rather than stored directly by Lagos Life.

Flutterwave and Bachs may process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Device and Technical Information

We may collect technical information including:

  • IP address;
  • browser type;
  • operating system;
  • device characteristics;
  • session information;
  • cookies;
  • security identifiers;
  • server logs;
  • error information;
  • request information; and
  • other technical signals required to operate and secure the Service.

Device Fingerprinting

Lagos Life uses device fingerprinting and related technical signals for security and abuse prevention.

Device fingerprinting may combine characteristics of a browser, device, session, network, or other technical environment in order to identify patterns associated with:

  • account farming;
  • fraud;
  • unauthorized automation;
  • bot activity;
  • repeated abuse;
  • circumvention of restrictions;
  • suspicious registrations; and
  • security threats.

Device fingerprinting is used for platform security and abuse prevention, not to convert a player's real-world identity into an advertising profile.

Advertising and Partnership Information

Where a person or business purchases advertising, sponsorship, placement, or another commercial service, we may process:

  • contact information;
  • business information;
  • campaign details;
  • creative materials;
  • transaction records;
  • communications;
  • placement information; and
  • performance or delivery information.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Lagos Life does not currently collect your real-world GPS location for gameplay.

A player's location within Lagos Life is a fictional in-game location and should not be confused with the player's physical location.

We do not currently provide social login.

We do not currently use GIF search or audio chat as part of Lagos Life's social features.

We may process information to:

  • create and manage accounts;
  • authenticate users;
  • save game progress;
  • operate the virtual economy;
  • process and reconcile purchases;
  • enable player-to-player interactions;
  • provide social features;
  • prevent fraud and abuse;
  • detect bots and automation;
  • protect account security;
  • investigate reports;
  • moderate content;
  • enforce the Terms of Use;
  • prevent unauthorized access;
  • operate advertising and partnerships;
  • respond to support requests;
  • maintain technical infrastructure;
  • diagnose errors;
  • improve gameplay;
  • balance the game economy;
  • comply with applicable laws;
  • establish or defend legal claims; and
  • protect Lagos Life, its players, and third parties.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, Lagos Life may rely on one or more lawful bases permitted under Nigerian data protection law.

These may include:

Contract

Processing necessary to create your account, provide Lagos Life, save your game, process purchases, deliver paid services, and provide features you request.

Legitimate Interests

Processing reasonably necessary for interests including:

  • security;
  • fraud prevention;
  • abuse prevention;
  • protection of the virtual economy;
  • service improvement;
  • moderation;
  • transaction reconciliation; and
  • protecting the rights of Lagos Life and its users,

where those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.

Legal Obligation

Processing required to comply with applicable law, lawful requests, accounting requirements, regulatory obligations, or court orders.

Consent

Where consent is the appropriate legal basis, including certain non-essential cookies or future optional features, we will request consent where required.

Consent may be withdrawn where applicable.

6. Cookies

Lagos Life uses cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies are small pieces of information stored or accessed through your browser or device.

Necessary Cookies

Necessary cookies may be used for purposes such as:

  • account sessions;
  • authentication;
  • security;
  • fraud prevention;
  • load management;
  • preferences required for requested functionality; and
  • maintaining core game functions.

Disabling necessary cookies may prevent parts of Lagos Life from functioning correctly.

Preference Cookies

Where used, preference cookies may remember settings or choices you make.

Analytics or Other Non-Essential Cookies

If Lagos Life deploys non-essential analytics, measurement, advertising, or similar cookies, their purposes will be identified through the relevant cookie notice or preference interface.

Where applicable law requires consent, users will be provided with an appropriate choice to accept or reject non-essential cookies.

Similar Technologies

Technologies that perform functions similar to cookies may be treated consistently with applicable privacy requirements.

Security-oriented device fingerprinting is separately described in this Policy.

7. Advertising

Advertising inside Lagos Life is primarily integrated into the fictional game world.

It may include:

  • billboards;
  • banners;
  • branded venues;
  • branded products;
  • virtual applications;
  • sponsored activities;
  • branded clothing or consumables;
  • environmental placements; and
  • similar game integrations.

Lagos Life does not currently use a player's real-world GPS location for advertising.

We do not currently describe the game as using behavioural advertising based on external browsing activity.

If this changes, this Policy and any required consent mechanisms will be updated.

8. How We Share Information

We may share personal information where reasonably necessary with service providers and other recipients described below.

DigitalOcean

Lagos Life's infrastructure is hosted using DigitalOcean.

Information may therefore be stored or processed using DigitalOcean infrastructure and related infrastructure services.

Flutterwave and Bachs

Flutterwave processes payments and related transaction information (Bachs did so for payments made before 6 October 2026).

We may exchange information with Flutterwave and Bachs as necessary to:

  • initiate payments;
  • confirm payment status;
  • reconcile transactions;
  • investigate failed purchases;
  • detect fraud;
  • process lawful refunds; and
  • resolve payment disputes.

Service Providers

We may use additional technical service providers where reasonably necessary to operate Lagos Life.

Such providers should receive only information reasonably necessary for their role and remain subject to applicable contractual and legal obligations.

Legal and Safety Reasons

We may preserve or disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

  • comply with applicable law;
  • respond to lawful court orders or government requests;
  • investigate fraud or crime;
  • protect the security of Lagos Life;
  • enforce our Terms;
  • protect users or third parties; or
  • establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Business Changes

If ownership or operation of Lagos Life is reorganized, transferred, incorporated, sold, or otherwise restructured, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

9. International Processing

DigitalOcean, Flutterwave, Bachs, and other service providers may operate infrastructure or process information in jurisdictions outside Nigeria.

Where personal data is transferred internationally, Lagos Life will seek to use transfer mechanisms and safeguards required by applicable Nigerian data protection law.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and for legitimate legal, security, accounting, transaction, dispute, and fraud-prevention requirements.

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods.

Factors affecting retention may include:

  • whether the account remains active;
  • whether a transaction must be reconciled;
  • fraud-prevention requirements;
  • security requirements;
  • legal limitation periods;
  • disputes;
  • enforcement history;
  • applicable legal obligations; and
  • whether information can instead be anonymized.

11. Account Deletion

Lagos Life supports soft deletion of accounts.

When an account is soft-deleted, it may become inaccessible to the player while some records remain in our systems for a limited period or for legitimate purposes.

We may retain information where reasonably necessary for:

  • transaction ledgers;
  • fraud prevention;
  • abuse prevention;
  • payment reconciliation;
  • chargeback handling;
  • security;
  • moderation history;
  • legal compliance;
  • dispute resolution; or
  • legal claims.

Where continued retention is no longer necessary, personal information will be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable law.

Deletion of an account does not necessarily require deletion of information that we are legally entitled or required to retain.

12. Transaction and Economy Records

Because Lagos Life contains a persistent player economy, certain transaction and economic records may need to be retained even after an account is deleted.

This helps preserve:

  • ledger integrity;
  • transfers involving other players;
  • fraud investigations;
  • economic consistency;
  • payment reconciliation; and
  • dispute records.

Where possible and appropriate, records may be pseudonymized or otherwise separated from directly identifying account information after deletion.

Messages sent through Lagos Life may be stored and processed to:

  • protect users; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Users should not assume that direct messages or venue communications are end-to-end encrypted unless Lagos Life expressly states otherwise.

Security systems may automatically identify activity as suspicious based on account, transaction, device, session, behavioural, or technical signals.

  • additional verification;
  • temporary restrictions;
  • transaction review;
  • rate limits;
  • abuse-prevention measures; or
  • referral for further review.

Where applicable law grants a right to request human intervention or contest a qualifying automated decision, Lagos Life will respect that right.
